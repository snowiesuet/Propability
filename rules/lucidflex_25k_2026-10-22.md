## 1. Checkout configurations

Compare all four configurations independently:

| Configuration | Evaluation consistency | Daily loss limit, evaluation and sim-funded | Funded payout timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DLL on / 40% | 40% | On | On-demand available |
| DLL on / 50% | 50% add-on | On | On-demand available |
| DLL off / 40% | 40% | Off | Regular end-of-day schedule |
| DLL off / 50% | 50% add-on | Off | Regular end-of-day schedule |

The selections are fixed for the active account. DLL-on is a cheaper purchase configuration; 50% consistency costs extra. The exact prices, discounts, and add-on amounts have **not** been captured. DLL is a **soft session lockout**, not an account-ending breach. The dollar DLL amount and its precise calculation basis have not been confirmed for this snapshot. An intraday payout request pauses funded trading until the end of the trading day. The stated on-demand session window is 6:00 p.m. to 3:45 p.m. ET; hedging violations remove intraday payout eligibility.

Source: [LucidFlex customization](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/16226050-lucidflex-customization).

## 2. Evaluation

| Rule | $25K value |
| --- | --- |
| Starting nominal balance | $25,000 |
| Profit target | $1,250; target balance $26,250 |
| Maximum loss allowance | $1,000, EOD trailing |
| Maximum position | 2 minis **or** 20 micros |
| Scaling restriction | None during evaluation |
| Consistency | Checkout selection: 40% or 50% |
| Daily loss limit | Checkout selection: on or off |
| Purchase fee | One-time; amount unconfirmed |
| Monthly rebilling | None |
| Evaluation deadline | No pass deadline stated; separate inactivity policies still apply |
| Funded activation fee | $0 |

The target and consistency requirement must both be satisfied before passing; hitting the dollar target alone is insufficient. Source states 40% can pass in as few as three days, 50% in as few as two; this does not establish a separate minimum-day rule independent of consistency.

Sources: [Evaluation account](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12945790-lucidflex-evaluation-account), [scaling plan](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12945808-lucidflex-scaling-plan).

### Evaluation consistency

For positive total evaluation profit:

`consistency = largest single winning-day profit / total evaluation profit`

The ratio must be at or below the selected limit. Exceeding it delays eligibility; continue earning profit until compliant. Losses reduce total profit and can therefore worsen the ratio.

At exactly $1,250 profit, the strict mathematical limits are $500 for 40% and $625 for 50%. The published **50% add-on** example permits $650 through a cushion. The page says the cushion varies proportionally with profit; it is not a universal fixed $25 allowance. Its complete formula/rounding policy is not specified here. Do not apply that cushion to 40% by inference or silently encode 52% as a confirmed general rule.

Source: [Consistency percentage](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12945805-lucidflex-consistency-percentage).

## 3. Evaluation and sim-funded drawdown

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Drawdown type | End-of-day trailing maximum loss limit (MLL) |
| Drawdown distance | $1,000 |
| Initial loss floor | $24,000 |
| Published initial trail balance | $26,100 |
| Locked loss floor | $25,100 |
| Payout-request effect | Adjust/lock MLL to $25,100 |
| Breach boundary | Reaching the loss floor breaches the account; equality is not safe |

The floor ratchets upward from the highest end-of-session closing balance and does not move downward after losses. The published wording says it locks once balance **exceeds** the initial trail balance. Distinguish updating the trailing floor at EOD from monitoring whether the account touches that floor during trading. Exact intraday realized/unrealized-equity enforcement and equality/rounding at the trail-lock threshold need confirmation before implementing an authoritative simulator.

Illustration: $27,000 less a $1,000 requested payout leaves $26,000, which is $900 above the locked $25,100 floor. The trader receives $900 after the 90/10 split; the account deduction is the $1,000 request, not the $900 receipt.

Source: [LucidFlex drawdown](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12945815-lucidflex-drawdown).

## 4. Sim-funded account and scaling

- Maximum loss allowance remains $1,000 EOD trailing; no consistency requirement.
- Daily loss limit selection carries over from evaluation.
- Starting limit: 1 mini or 10 micros at $0–$999 profit.
- At $1,000 profit, the limit becomes 2 minis or 20 micros.
- Scaling updates at **session end**, not immediately when intraday profit crosses a threshold.
- Limits can move down as profits decrease, including after a payout.
- Mini/micro limits are alternatives, not additive allowances. Mixed-position equivalence needs confirmation if added later.

Sources: [Funded account](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12945795-lucidflex-funded-account), [scaling plan](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12945808-lucidflex-scaling-plan).

## 5. Sim-funded payouts

| Requirement | $25K rule |
| --- | --- |
| Qualifying days | Five separate days earning at least $100 each per payout cycle |
| Cycle profitability | Positive net profit for that cycle |
| Minimum request | $500 |
| Maximum request | 50% of current account profit, capped at $1,000 |
| Trader share | 90%; firm share 10% |
| Day-count reset | Five qualifying days must be earned again after each approved payout |
| Maximum simulated-funded payouts | Five per account |
| Separate payout buffer requirement | None stated |

The 50% cap uses **profit**, not half the nominal $25,000 account balance. A $1,000 request pays the trader $900. A separate absence of payout-buffer requirements does not remove the MLL or its payout-triggered lock. Eligibility may be lost if trading reduces the required balance before processing. Requests are final; payment processing/approval is not guaranteed to be instantaneous. The published disbursement estimate is within two business days after approval.

Source: [LucidFlex payouts](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12945796-lucidflex-payouts). Timing depends on the checkout configuration in section 1.

## 6. Live stage — reference only

Live transition is discretionary; five sim payouts are a maximum, not a promise that transition waits until payout five. Eligible funded accounts need at least one payout to move; remaining simulated accounts close at transition.

For a $25K-origin live account: start at $0 with $1,000 EOD drawdown, daily payouts, no DLL, and no consistency requirement. The MLL locks at +$100 after earning the starting drawdown amount or upon an earlier payout request. The initial -$1,000 floor is an inference from the stated starting balance and drawdown.

The repeatable bonus requires $2,000 live profit and 20 qualifying trading days; gross bonus is $1,000, subject to a 90/10 split. The page defines a day using at least one trade and end-of-session net-of-fees P&L of +$1/-$1. Payouts do not reduce progress toward its profit target. Exact reset mechanics need confirmation before modeling repeated bonuses.

Source: [New live structure](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/16558826-new-live-structure).

For CME products including MNQ/NQ, live sizing starts at 1 mini or 10 micros, rising to 2 minis or 20 micros at $2,000 profit; tiers update EOD. COMEX starts at 0 minis/5 micros and rises to 1 mini/10 micros at that threshold. Source: [Live scaling](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/15245873-live-scaling-plan).

## 7. Trading hours, permitted activity, and commissions

- For evaluation/sim-funded, close positions by 4:45 p.m. Eastern; trading resumes at 6:00 p.m. Eastern. Observe earlier holiday closes. Lucid automatically closes remaining positions at the cutoff; the detailed policy explicitly says holding past that time does **not itself fail the account**. Include resulting execution costs/P&L rather than treating the cutoff as an automatic hard breach.
- Both the FAQ and detailed hours page write EST. Seasonal/DST interpretation still needs confirmation. For live accounts, the detailed policy prohibits swing trading and lists a 4:45 p.m. cutoff on Tradovate or 4:15 p.m. on Rithmic, with onboarding-specific risk settings. This conflicts with the general FAQ's live overnight-holding statement; preserve the conflict rather than silently permitting overnight live positions.
- News trading is permitted on Flex. Automated strategies and copiers are permitted but remain subject to the other trading rules.
- MNQ commission is **$0.50 per contract per side**, or **$1.00 per round trip**. This is a simulated-account P&L cost, separate from evaluation purchase fees.
- This reference does not claim to enumerate every prohibited practice, account limit, inactivity rule, or provider agreement restriction.

Sources: [Allowed trading times](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/11404729-allowed-trading-times), [general FAQ](https://lucidtrading.com/general-faq/), [permitted activities](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/11404728-other-trading-activities), [approved products and commissions](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/11508978-approved-products-and-commissions).
